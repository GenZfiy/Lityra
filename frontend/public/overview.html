Why LARE

Value for everyone in the loop

The same platform pays off differently for the people who learn on it, the institutions that run it, and the companies that hire through it.

For Students
Learn faster, prove your skills
  • Personalised path — a Cognitive Twin adapts to your strengths & gaps.
  • AI tutor & micro-lessons generated on any topic, on demand.
  • Real coding practice with instant feedback & adaptive drills.
  • Peer mesh — learn by teaching classmates a step behind.
  • Skill wallet & certificates that are verifiable and shareable.
  • Career readiness matched to real opportunities.
For Colleges
One platform, full visibility
  • All-in-one — curriculum, assessments, coding & certification together.
  • Live analytics — cohort performance, rankings & weak areas.
  • Proctored exams with anti-cheat flags on every answer page.
  • Curriculum Studio to author AI-assisted lesson material.
  • Placement bridge — Lityra Hire connects students to recruiters.
  • Lower cost — replaces several separate tools & licenses.
For Startups
Hire on proven skill
  • Skills-to-opportunity matching from verified profiles.
  • Build tests fast — AI-generated coding & aptitude question banks.
  • Proctored at scale with anti-cheat, so scores are trustworthy.
  • Structured interviews with automated evaluation.
  • Hire for ability, not just resumes — evidence, not guesswork.
  • Elastic & affordable — grow capacity as you need it.

A clear path to scale

2

Go global

CDN / edge caching for fast access worldwide.
